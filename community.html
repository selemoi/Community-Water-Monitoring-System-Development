Welcome to the Community Water Monitoring System

This platform helps track water quality in real time for safer, healthier communities.

About the Project

The Community Water Monitoring System is designed to measure and display water quality parameters such as pH, turbidity, and water level using sensors and IoT technologies.

Objectives

  • Provide real-time monitoring of water sources.
  • Alert users about unsafe water conditions.
  • Encourage environmental awareness and health.
